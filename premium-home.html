DATA ATLAS / 06

Know what
you are seeing.

EarthPulse keeps observation, derivation and demonstration visually separate. Scientific interfaces should make provenance understandable.

OBSERVEDDirect source data. Use when the interface represents an actual measured or published observation.
MODELEDDerived estimate or calculated indicator. Not the same as a direct measurement.
INFERREDAnalytical conclusion built from available information and assumptions.
DEMOSimulated visualization or example target used for interaction and product demonstration.
