## One-line description

EarthPulse is an interactive planetary intelligence platform for exploring earthquakes, air quality, space weather and other Earth-system signals through maps, analytics and source-aware visualizations.

## Short project description

EarthPulse brings Earth-system information into one interface: seismic activity, air quality, space weather and planetary exploration. The project emphasizes clear provenance and separates observed data, derived calculations, analytical interpretation and demonstration layers.

## Developer / maker story

### How I built EarthPulse: an Earth intelligence dashboard

I wanted to build an interface where Earth science information feels connected instead of scattered across unrelated dashboards.

EarthPulse combines interactive 3D Earth exploration with data-oriented pages for earthquakes, air quality, space weather and planetary analytics. A major design principle is transparency: an observed measurement should not look identical to a calculated indicator or a demonstration visualization.

The platform is built as a web application and uses public Earth-science data sources where connected. Its current experience includes Planet Pulse, Analytics, Data Atlas and Mission Control, along with dedicated answer-first pages that explain how the earthquake, space-weather and air-quality features work.

The interesting engineering problems are less about making another dashboard and more about connecting different data shapes into one understandable model, keeping source context visible, isolating unavailable feeds, and presenting scientific information without pretending that a visualization is more authoritative than its underlying source.
