3.1 Why cookies may be used

  • Essential operation, security and preferences.
  • Analytics and performance measurement.
  • Advertising delivery, frequency control and measurement.

3.2 Google advertising cookies

When Google ads are displayed, Google and its partners may place or read cookies on a user's browser. Google states that advertising cookies can be used to support relevant advertising, reporting and related functions. Users can control personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.

3.3 Managing cookies

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect some site features or advertising behavior.

3.4 Consent where required

Where applicable law or an advertising-platform policy requires consent for cookies, local storage or personalized advertising, the required consent mechanism should be used before the relevant processing occurs.
