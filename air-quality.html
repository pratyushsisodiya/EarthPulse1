How does EarthPulse track air quality?

EarthPulse is a source-aware Earth intelligence interface designed to turn available air-quality observations into a readable, comparable view. When air-quality feeds are connected, the platform can organize station or regional readings, pollutant context, timestamps, location and source provenance while keeping measured observations distinct from modeled or derived indicators.

OBSERVATION CONTEXTPOLLUTANTS + LOCATIONFRESHNESS + SOURCE

What EarthPulse does

  • Groups air-quality observations into a common interface instead of forcing users to interpret multiple provider formats.
  • Keeps location, timestamp, pollutant and source context next to the reading.
  • Supports regional or station-level views when those fields exist in the connected feed.
  • Labels calculated or analytical indicators separately from direct measurements.
  • Shows missing or stale data explicitly instead of fabricating a current reading.

What to look at

Pollutant

Identify which measured pollutant or index the reading represents.

Location

Check the station, region or geographic scope of the reading.

Timestamp

Confirm when the observation was measured or retrieved.

Source

Trace the reading back to the provider or dataset.

Observation vs interpretation

LayerMeaning

DemonstrationInteractive UI content that is not being presented as live measurement data.

Important distinction

An air-quality reading describes the conditions represented by a particular source, location and time. EarthPulse should not turn one station or region into a claim about the entire planet, and any broader interpretation should be labeled as analytical rather than measured fact.
